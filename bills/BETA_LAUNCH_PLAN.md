# Diary Beta Launch Plan

## Target users

Recruit 20-40 people who already keep daily money records:

- 5-10 shopkeepers for Meri Dukaan.
- 5-10 electricians, plumbers, painters, and other service workers for Mera Kaam.
- 5-10 tutors or coaching teachers for Meri Class.
- 5-10 small business owners who need simple bills and payment records for Hisab Kitab.

Start with people who are comfortable giving honest feedback and can use the app for seven days.

## Beta goals

Learn whether users:

- Understand what Diary is for within five seconds.
- Select the right work type without help.
- Complete their first record without help.
- Return daily for one week.
- Trust local storage and backup guidance.
- Notice missing features or confusing words.
- Would recommend Diary to another business owner.

## Timeline

### Week 1: First 10 users

Onboard two or three people from each main group. Watch the first record being created. Record confusion and data-safety questions without collecting customer names, phone numbers, or amounts.

### Week 2: Remaining users

Onboard the rest of the 20-40 user group. Use the same short introduction and avoid teaching users where the interface is unclear.

### Weeks 3-4: Feedback and observation

Send one weekly check-in. Collect feedback from the app, the WhatsApp group, and direct conversations. Mark crashes and data-loss reports as critical immediately.

### Week 5: Analysis

Group feedback into critical bugs, confusing UX, missing core features, and nice-to-have requests. Fix trust and data-safety issues first. Defer features that do not serve the four current work types.

## Success signals

- At least 80% complete a first record without help.
- At least half return on four or more days in the first week.
- No unresolved data-loss or restore bugs.
- Users can explain where their data is stored and how to back it up.
- Each profession group can name one daily problem Diary solved.

## Privacy choice

Diary does not add analytics in this beta. Feedback is collected intentionally through the feedback page, direct conversations, and the tester WhatsApp group. Do not request or store customer names, phone numbers, payment references, or amounts in the feedback log.
