# BILLS User Guide

## Start

Open BILLS and choose the work type that matches your daily work. There is no login. Your choice opens the right dashboard.

## Meri Dukaan

Tap `+ Sale Likho` for money received by the shop. Tap `- Dukaan ka Kharcha` for an expense, choose its reason, and save it. The dashboard shows today's sale, expenses, and amount left.

## Mera Kaam

Create a job with the customer, work, total amount, and advance received. Use `Payment / expense` to add later payments or job costs. The job shows customer balance, expenses, and net amount. Mark it completed when the work is finished.

## Meri Class

Add a student, then record the expected fee and amount paid. The student card shows collected and pending amounts. Use `Add payment` for a later payment.

## Invoices

Choose `Mera Hisaab`, then create a bill. Add the customer and one or more items, review the total, and approve it. From the invoice page you can record payments, print or download the bill, and share it yourself through WhatsApp.

## Backup

Open Settings and choose `Download full backup` regularly, especially before changing phones or clearing browser data. To restore, select a JSON backup, review it, and confirm the import. Existing records with the same IDs are kept.
