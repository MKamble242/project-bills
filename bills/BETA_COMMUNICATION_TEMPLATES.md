## Welcome message

Namaste! You are invited to test Diary, a simple digital business diary for daily work.

Choose your work type, create one record, and use Diary for the next seven days. Your records are saved in the browser on this device. Open `Diary settings` and download a backup before changing phones or clearing browser data.

Please tell us what feels useful, what feels confusing, and anything that fails. Do not send customer names, phone numbers, payment references, or amounts in feedback.
