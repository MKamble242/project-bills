# Diary Beta Install Guide

Share this guide with every tester.

## Before you start

Diary is a simple digital business diary for daily records. No login is needed. In local mode, records stay in the browser on this device. Do not enter real customer information during testing unless you are comfortable keeping it on this phone.

## Open Diary

1. Open the Diary link in Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone.
2. Wait for the first screen to load.
3. Choose the work type that best matches your daily work:
   - Meri Dukaan for shop sales and expenses.
   - Mera Kaam for service jobs, payments, and job expenses.
   - Meri Class for students and fees.
   - Mera Hisaab for simple bills, customers, and payments.
4. Read the three-step quick start. Choose `Start using Diary` or `Skip for now`.

## Install on Android

1. Open Diary in Chrome.
2. Tap the three-dot menu.
3. Tap `Add to Home screen` or `Install app`.
4. Confirm the name `Diary`.
5. Open Diary from the new home-screen icon.

The exact wording can differ slightly by phone and Chrome version.

## Install on iPhone

1. Open Diary in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button.
3. Choose `Add to Home Screen`.
4. Confirm the name `Diary`.
5. Open Diary from the home screen.

## Create your first record

- Meri Dukaan: add one sale or expense.
- Mera Kaam: create one job with a customer, work, total amount, and advance.
- Meri Class: add one student and one fee record.
- Mera Hisaab: add one simple bill with a customer and work item.

Confirm that the saved record appears in the dashboard and remains after a refresh.

## Protect your data

Open `Diary settings` and choose `Download full backup` after your first few records. Keep the JSON backup somewhere safe before changing phones, clearing browser data, or uninstalling the browser. Diary does not automatically sync local records to the cloud.

## Send feedback

Open `Diary settings`, choose `Send feedback`, answer the two short questions, and tap `Send feedback via WhatsApp`. Do not include customer names, phone numbers, payment references, or amounts in feedback.

## What to report immediately

Report a crash, a blank screen, a record that disappears, a wrong total, or a restore problem as critical. Include the work type and what you were trying to do, but do not include private customer data.
