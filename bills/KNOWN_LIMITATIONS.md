# Known Limitations

- Local data belongs to one browser on one device. There is no cloud sync or multi-device account.
- Clearing browser data without a backup can remove local records.
- Payment entries are manual. Diary does not connect to banks or payment gateways.
- WhatsApp sharing opens a prefilled message; it does not send or report delivery.
- The app supports four focused diaries: Meri Dukaan, Mera Kaam, Meri Class, and Mere Kharche.
- Browser storage and installed PWA behavior depend on the browser and device.
- Very large histories are kept in one scrolling list; there is no pagination yet.
- Job payment and expense quick edits use a simple value prompt rather than a full editor.
