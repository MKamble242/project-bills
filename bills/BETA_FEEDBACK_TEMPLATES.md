# Diary Beta Feedback Templates

## Welcome message

Namaste! Diary is a simple digital business diary for your daily work. Choose your work type, add your first record, and come back tomorrow. Your records are saved on this device. Please download a backup in Settings before changing phones or clearing browser data.

## Weekly check-in

How was Diary this week?

1. What was most useful?
2. What was most confusing?
3. Did you use it on more than one day?
4. Did anything fail, disappear, or feel unsafe?
5. What is the one improvement you want next?

Please do not send customer names, phone numbers, payment references, or amounts in the reply.

## WhatsApp group message

Welcome to the Diary beta group. This group is for short product feedback and urgent bug reports. Please describe what you were trying to do, what you expected, and what happened. Do not share customer information or screenshots containing private business data.
